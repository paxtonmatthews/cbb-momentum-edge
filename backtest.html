Backtest the model

Replays finished games and checks the model's projections, made at minute 10, 20, 30, 35 and 38, against the real final scores. Good calibration means the "80% range" column sits near 80%. Lower Brier scores mean better win probabilities.

This checks projection accuracy, not betting profit. Profit needs historical live odds, which aren't in the free feed.
